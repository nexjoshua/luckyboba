Our Story

Little cups of good luck

Lucky Boba is a Taiwan-style boba milk tea, juice and coffee brand that believes every cup should feel like a small win. From food-truck carts to cozy bubble tea cafés, we bring the same quality everywhere.

  • Consistent quality — every branch is supplied by our own central warehouse.
  • Freshly made — teas brewed and pearls cooked throughout the day.
  • Made for sharing — kiosks, carts and cafés close to where you are.
